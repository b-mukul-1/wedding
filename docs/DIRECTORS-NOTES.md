# Creative direction: three different wedding films

## Intent

These are authored as *three short-form interactive wedding films*, not three generic landing pages. Everything is rooted in the family's real information, without fabricating a romantic biography, reception timing, or a fictional dress code. North Indian cultural motifs—Shri Ganesha, phoolon ki baarish, the mandap, seven blessings, illuminated palace architecture, and the warmth of two families—appear as story beats, not as a pile of ornaments.

## Film Two — The Living Letter

The editorial design is intentionally much quieter. It should feel like receiving a personal printed invitation designed by a couture stationery house, with whitespace as a core creative asset. The narrative sequence moves from addressed envelope and wax seal to sacred folio, personalized invitation, kinetic names, two portrait pages, families, calendar, written invitation, venue engraving and final personal note.

**Texture and motion:** simulated paper embossing, folding envelope flap, letter pullout, slow ink-style handwriting, separate editorial folios, restrained typography reveals and a hand-engraved palace motif. It is not a reskin of Royal and does not reuse the palace as a full-bleed backdrop.

## Film Three — The Enchanted Garden

Floral begins as a tranquil resort landscape, awakens in morning light and follows butterflies into a growing pathway. Later chapters stage a blessing shower, individual bride and groom reveals, a slow union, a garden mandap, family blessings, a floral date reveal and a sunset venue finale.

**Nature animation:** shader-powered atmospheric depth on desktop, responsive CSS butterflies and floating blooms, parallax scenery, SVG flowers, bright sun halos, and expanding vines. Animation is fluid but capped to preserve mobile performance.

## Art direction

- Royal: sapphire midnight, antique champagne gold, deep burgundy and warm carved sandstone.
- Minimal: warm parchment, oxblood wax, muted rose-taupe ink and couture editorial composition.
- Floral: coral dawn, champagne cream, dusty garden pink, misty sage and soft copper accents.
- Every visual is an illustrative interpretation, not an image of the couple or real venue.

## To reach actual film-studio character animation

The architecture deliberately leaves room for real character animation. Replace the illustrative PNGs with rigged 3D `.glb` characters or layered Rive rigs, then drive their clip progress from the provided chapter ScrollTriggers. Commercial-grade gait cycles, arm throws, fabric simulation, custom facial acting and synced orchestral/shehnai music are separate production assets that require specialist artistry and licensed audio, not just a framework switch.

## Accessibility

The scroll story remains readable without sound, sound is never required, motion is toned down or disabled for visitors who request reduced motion, and primary actions can be activated with keyboard or touch.
